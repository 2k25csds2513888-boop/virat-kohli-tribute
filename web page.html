Biography Of Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli: The King Who Never Stopped Chasing Virat Kohli is one of the greatest cricketers of the modern era. Known for his aggressive mindset, unmatched fitness, and incredible consistency, his journey from the streets of Delhi to becoming one of cricket's biggest icons is a story of determination, sacrifice, and relentless hard work.

  • Full Name: Virat Kohli
  • Born: November 5, 1988
  • Birthplace: Delhi, India
  • Parents: Prem Kohli and Saroj Kohli

    • Virat developed a love for cricket at a very young age. Recognizing his talent, his father enrolled him in the West Delhi Cricket Academy when he was just nine years old. His coaches quickly realized that he possessed exceptional talent and an intense desire to improve. A Defining Moment In 2006, tragedy struck when Virat's father passed away during an important Ranji Trophy match. Despite the devastating loss, Virat chose to bat for Delhi the very next day and scored a courageous 90 runs. That innings became a symbol of his mental strength and dedication to cricket. Rise to Stardom Virat's breakthrough came in 2008 when he captained India to victory in the ICC Under-19 Cricket World Cup. Later that year, he made his ODI debut for India against Sri Lanka. Initially, he fought hard to secure a permanent place in the national team, but consistent performances soon made him impossible to ignore. Becoming "The Chase Master" Over the next few years, Virat became famous for his ability to chase difficult targets. His fearless batting, quick running between the wickets, and hunger for centuries earned him the nickname "The Chase Master."

    Some of his memorable achievements include:
  • 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup winner
  • Multiple ICC awards
  • Dozens of international centuries
  • Numerous batting records in ODIs and Tests
  • Becoming one of the fastest players to several run milestones

    Virat became captain of the Indian team across formats and transformed the team's culture. Under his leadership: India became one of the fittest teams in world cricket. Fast bowling became a major strength. India achieved its first-ever Test series victory in Australia (2018-19). India dominated the ICC Test rankings for several years. Personal Life Virat married Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma in December 2017. They are among India's most admired celebrity couples and have two children. Both are also involved in philanthropy and various social causes. Virat Kohli's legacy extends beyond statistics. He changed how modern cricketers approach: Fitness Mental toughness Professionalism Passion for representing their country His story proves that talent alone is not enough—discipline, resilience, and an unyielding desire to improve are what create greatness. "Self-belief and hard work will always earn you success." — Virat Kohli
